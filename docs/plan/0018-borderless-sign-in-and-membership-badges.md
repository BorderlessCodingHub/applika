## Contract
- `POST /auth/borderless/signin` `{"email": str ≤100, "password": str 1..200}` → 200 `{"redirect_to": str|null, "new_account": bool}` (+ `__access`, `__refresh` on the web branch only) | 401 `{"detail": "Invalid email or password"}` | 403 `{"detail": "Verify your email on Borderless first", "code": "email_unverified"}` | 409 `{"detail": str, "code": "binding_conflict"|"email_ambiguous"}` | 422 (invalid body) | 422 `{"detail": "This Borderless email cannot be used with applika: it must be a valid address of at most 100 characters", "code": "email_unusable"}` (no binding for the Borderless id and its email is over 100 characters or not a valid address; refused before any write, not counted by the per-email limit) | 429 `{"detail": "Too many sign-in attempts. Try again in <n> seconds."}` + `Retry-After` | 503 `{"detail": "Borderless is unavailable, try again later"}` or `{"detail": "Borderless sign-in is not configured"}`.
- `GET|PATCH /users/me` adds `"badges": [{"badge": "borderless_base"|"borderless_psp", "granted_at": str}]`, `"borderless": {"status": "active"|"expired"|"needs_reconnect", "expires_at": str, "email": str}|null` (`status` computed server-side at request time; `expired` = `access_token_expires_at` past; a badge is held only while `active`), `"github_restore": {"available": bool, "prompt": bool}`; `badges` lists live rows, held or not; `github_id` int|null; removes `discord`, `discord_linked`. CLI 0.1.4..1.1.0 read only `username`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`.
- `POST /auth/borderless/confirm` (signed in) `{"email": str ≤100, "password": str 1..200}` → 204, session and cookies untouched | 401 `Not authenticated` / `{"detail": "Invalid email or password"}` | 403 `email_unverified` | 409 `{"detail": "This is not the Borderless account linked to your applika account", "code": "binding_conflict"}` | 422 | 429 | 503 (same bodies as signin).

## Risks
- Every refresh calls Borderless while the token lives (web about every 10 min, CLI on expiry): refresh latency and Borderless 429s. Bounded by `BORDERLESS_REFRESH_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, no retry; a failure only stamps `profile_read_failed_at`. The row lock serialises concurrent reads.
- Credential proxy: the password rides a request body. TLS only, https enforced in PROD, caplog test, no body-logging middleware.
- B deployed before A's migration → 500 everywhere; runbook order is the only guard. Borderless down at B deploy → nobody can sign in.
- A→B gap: GitHub signups and new Discord webhooks after A have no copies until C1 tops them up; such a user whose emails differ cannot restore before C.
- Rolling back B once Borderless-only users exist: pre-B `AdminUser.github_id: int` 500s `/admin/users`, and those users cannot sign in.
- Weekly friction (owner's choice "Lock reports until re-sign-in"): the Borderless token dies 7 days after a password sign-in and cannot be renewed, so every reporting member types their Borderless password about once a week to keep reports; members who only track applications are not blocked. A Borderless account without a password cannot sign in at all until it sets one on Borderless.
- Email match binds any GitHub-era account with the same email; trust rests on Borderless's verification. Key lost → every binding `needs_reconnect`, no badge counts until each member confirms; owner backs the key up.
